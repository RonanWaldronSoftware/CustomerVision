EMAIL VERIFICATION SETUP

Sender: enter your own Gmail address during setup
Provider: Gmail / Google Workspace

1. Make sure Two-Step Verification is enabled for your-email@gmail.com.
2. Open https://myaccount.google.com/apppasswords
3. Create an app password named CustomerVision.
4. Double-click START_CUSTOMERVISION.
5. Enter your Gmail sender address and its 16-character app password when asked.

Use the Google app password, not the normal Gmail password.
The password is saved only in the local .env file beside CustomerVision.
Do not share the .env file.

If App Passwords is unavailable, the Google Workspace administrator may have disabled it.
