BRITEBIN CUSTOMERVISION

WINDOWS: Double-click START_CUSTOMERVISION.bat
MAC:     Double-click START_CUSTOMERVISION.command
LINUX:   Run START_CUSTOMERVISION.sh

FIRST LAUNCH:
CustomerVision asks for your Gmail sender address and its Google app password.
Use a Google app password, not the normal Gmail password.

The dashboard then opens automatically in your web browser.
Keep the launcher window open while using CustomerVision.

Requirement: Node.js LTS must be installed.
